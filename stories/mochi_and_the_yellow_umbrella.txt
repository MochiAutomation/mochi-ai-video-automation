STORY TITLE: Mochi and the Yellow Umbrella

TARGET AGE: 2–5 years

MAIN CHARACTER:
Mochi – A fluffy blue bunny with floppy pink-lined ears, big green eyes and a yellow umbrella.

THEME:
Kindness and helping others.

SCENE 1:
Mochi walks happily through a beautiful garden holding a yellow umbrella.

SCENE 2:
Mochi sees a little bird sitting under a tree. The bird looks sad because it is raining.

SCENE 3:
Mochi gently shares the yellow umbrella with the little bird.

SCENE 4:
The rain stops. A beautiful rainbow appears in the sky. Mochi and the bird happily play together.

SCENE 5:
Mochi smiles at the children and says:
"Always be kind and help others!"

MORAL:
Sharing and kindness make everyone happy.
